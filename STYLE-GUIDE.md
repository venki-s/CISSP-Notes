# Notes Style Guide

## Editing principles

- **Accuracy first:** verify disputed definitions and distinguish illustrative shortcuts from strict definitions.
- **Concise but sufficient:** one clear explanation, followed by short examples, formulas, or tables where beneficial.
- **Exam language vs. real world:** label differences explicitly, rather than replacing one with the other.
- **Emphasize distinctions:** highlight similar-sounding terms, responsibilities, and sequence/order questions.
- **Track provenance:** record book title, chapter/section, NIST or ISC2 document links where applicable.
- **No unsupported certainty:** tag ambiguous questions for review.
- **Copyright:** paraphrase and synthesize; do not reproduce substantial portions of licensed materials.

## Suggested chapter structure

1. Core concepts and definitions
2. Frameworks, standards, and processes
3. Formulas, models, and tables
4. Key distinctions and comparisons
5. Brief practical examples
6. CISSP exam traps and common misconceptions
7. Five-minute revision checklist
8. Sources and open questions

> Remove empty sections as notes evolve; do not preserve template text in finalized notes.
