### 2.2 BCP Team Composition

- **Department representatives:** Represent core business services.
- **Business unit members:** Represent key functional areas.
- **IT specialists:** Provide technical expertise for continuity planning.
- **Cybersecurity team:** Guide the BCP process and security requirements.
- **Facilities and physical security:** Address buildings and physical infrastructure.
- **Legal counsel:** Address legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.
- **Human resources:** Address staffing and employee impacts.
- **Public relations:** Plan stakeholder and public communications during disruptions.
- **Senior management:** Set priorities, provide direction, and allocate resources.
