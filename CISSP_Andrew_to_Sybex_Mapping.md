## Recommended workflow

1. Follow Andrew course **section order** within each CISSP domain.
2. Review the corresponding Sybex chapter notes before the section quiz.
3. Record incorrect questions and revise the existing chapter notes only for genuine gaps.
4. After completing all domain sections, begin timed practice exams.
